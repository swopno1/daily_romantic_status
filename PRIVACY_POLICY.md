## 1. Introduction

ViveScript Solutions LLC ("we", "us", or "our") built the **Daily Romantic Status** application as a free, privacy-first mobile utility. This Privacy Policy informs users regarding our practices and policies concerning the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using our application.

By using Daily Romantic Status, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Daily Romantic Status is designed from the ground up to respect your personal privacy:

* **No User Accounts:** We do not require registration, usernames, passwords, or emails.
* **No Personal Data:** We do not collect your name, phone number, address, contacts, or financial details.
* **No Device Sensors or Biometrics:** We do not access camera, microphone, GPS location, or device files.
* **No Analytics or Trackers:** We do not embed behavioral tracking SDKs or user tracking analytics.

---

## 3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Any preferences or saved favorites you create (such as marking a quote with a heart icon or toggling hashtag visibility) are stored strictly **locally on your device** using Android's standard private application sandbox storage (`SharedPreferences`). This data never leaves your device and is erased completely if you uninstall the app or clear app data in system settings.

---

## 4. Network Communication & Advertising

### 4.1 Local Content Generation
All romantic statuses, daily picks, and AI-style combinatorial captions are synthesized entirely **offline on your device** using our built-in algorithms. No internet connection is needed for status generation, copying, or bookmarking.

### 4.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)
The application includes configuration for Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. During this release and development period, the app runs using **Google's official test ad identifiers**. 

When production advertisements are active, Google Mobile Ads may collect standard network identifiers, IP address, and advertising identifiers solely to deliver and measure ad placements in compliance with Google Play's Families and Advertising policies. For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review Google's Privacy Policy at: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any personal user data with third parties. When you tap "Share", the application invokes Android's standard system share sheet (`Intent.ACTION_SEND`), passing the selected quote text directly to whatever social app you select (e.g., WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook).

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Daily Romantic Status does not address anyone under the age of 13 knowingly, nor does it collect personally identifiable information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

---

## 7. Security

We value your trust. Since Daily Romantic Status does not store personal data on remote servers, your risk of personal data breaches through our app is non-existent.
